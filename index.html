Our People, Our Story, Our Values

Our Story

Lomiiee was created by healers who believe mountains awaken transformation. With backgrounds in psychology, meditation, and nature based therapy, we built a refuge where people can slow down, face what matters, and rise renewed.

Our Mission

To guide deep healing through nature, stillness, and therapeutic presence. We help you reconnect with yourself, with others, and with the earth.
